# Editorial and church inclusion principles (draft)

OneOnlineChurch is a discovery platform, not an ecclesiastical authority. Listing is not endorsement. Christian traditions and theological differences should be described accurately and respectfully, using both a church's self-description and clearly labelled directory categories. No church is marked verified until an authorised representative and official sources are checked. Allow disputes, corrections, appeals and removal requests.

Only publish authorised and attributable media. Do not scrape private meetings, circumvent embedding restrictions or describe scheduled video as confirmed live. No sensitive prayer requests, child messaging or unsupported crisis care at launch.

**Not yet approved:** governance council review, legal assessment, copyright agreements, privacy and safeguarding sign-off.
