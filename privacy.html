Privacy in development

OneOnlineChurch is an early-stage discovery prototype. The current public frontend does not offer accounts, prayer requests, or community chat. Searches and filters operate locally in your browser and are not transmitted by the application to an account system.

Normal hosting infrastructure may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, according to the hosting provider's configuration. A controller-specific privacy notice, retention policy and contact details must be approved before launch.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information to unverified contact routes.
