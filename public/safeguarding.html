Safety & safeguarding

Our public prototype does not provide peer messaging, prayer requests, mentoring, counselling, or child accounts. These features will not launch until trained responsible people, clear procedures, moderation and escalation routes are established.

OneOnlineChurch is not an emergency or crisis response service. If you are in immediate danger in the UK call 999; elsewhere contact local emergency services. Any future contact details and reporting mechanisms will be published only once staffed and verified.
